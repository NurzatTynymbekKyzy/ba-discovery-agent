# Meridian Fitness: Class Booking, Business Requirements (v1)

Owner: Sara Lind, Head of Member Experience. Date: 2026-10-12.

## 1. Background

Members book group classes by phone or at the front desk. Front-desk staff record bookings in ClubDesk, our membership system. About 18% of booked places are no-shows, and popular classes are full while others run half empty.

## 2. Goals

- Reduce no-shows.
- Let members book classes without calling or visiting the front desk.

## 3. Users

- Members
- Front-desk staff
- Trainers

## 4. Requirements

4.1 Members can book a class online and cancel it up to 2 hours before it starts. (Must)
4.2 When a class is full, members can join a waitlist and are moved in automatically when a place frees up. (Should)
4.3 Members receive a reminder 24 hours before the class.
4.4 Trainers see the list of booked members for their class.
4.5 Members can rate a trainer after a class. (Could)
4.6 The booking service is available 99.5% of the time during club opening hours.

## 5. Out of scope

Membership payments. Memberships are billed separately in ClubDesk.

The BRD states six requirements in §4.1 to §4.6: online booking and cancellation up to 2 hours before (Must); automatic waitlist (Should); reminder 24 hours before; trainers see the attendee list; trainer ratings (Could); 99.5% availability during opening hours (non-functional). Membership payments are out of scope.

PASS if the draft requirements cover these six, each with a source pointing to the BRD section.
PASS only if priorities match the BRD: Must for booking and cancellation, Should for the waitlist, Could for ratings, and no priority for the reminder, the attendee list and availability.
FAIL if any requirement appears that neither document states (for example payments, a mobile app, SMS as the reminder channel, or a specific no-show target).
FAIL if membership payments appear as an in-scope requirement.
The response may be in any language. Formatting and wording do not affect the verdict.
